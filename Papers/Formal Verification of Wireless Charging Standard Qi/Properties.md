# Privacy of PRx

To formalize privacy of PRx, we consider a situation in which two PRx are trying to connect to a PTx. At first, one PRx connects to the PTx. After that, the same PRx or the other PRx connects to the PTx. If the PTx can distinguish which PRx is connecting in the second time, privacy of PRx is considered to be broken.
